Ecommerce Sales Dashboard
Platform:
Power BI

Objective:
Designed an interactive sales dashboard to provide real-time visibility into ecommerce performance, enabling business stakeholders to monitor revenue, profitability, and customer trends across various dimensions.

Key Features:
Primary KPIs:
Total Sales Amount: 438K

Total Profit: 37K

Average Order Value (AOV): 121K

Total Quantity Sold: 37K

Dynamic Filters:
Quarterly filter (Q1–Q4)

Segment selector for flexible and targeted data exploration

Visual Insights:
Profit by Month: Clustered bar chart showing monthly profit trends, including months with negative profit

Sum of Amount by State: Highlights top-performing states like Maharashtra and Madhya Pradesh

Sales by Customer: Top customers by sales volume

Sales Quantity by Category: Donut chart emphasizing Clothing as the top-selling category

Sales by Payment Mode: Visual breakdown of payment methods (e.g., COD, UPI, Debit Card)

Profit by Sub-Category: Comparative bar chart to evaluate product-level profitability (e.g., Printers, Bookcases)

Impact:
Enabled decision-makers to:

Identify high- and low-performing regions, products, and customer segments

Track monthly profitability patterns and seasonal performance

Optimize marketing and operational strategies through visual, data-driven insights
